<section id="what-is-gpa">
  <h2>What is GPA?</h2>
  <p>
    Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis is a rare autoimmune disease causing inflammation in small- to medium-sized blood vessels—most commonly affecting the sinuses, lungs, and kidneys. While serious, early diagnosis and targeted therapies allow many people to achieve long-term remission.
  </p>
</section>

<section id="patient-support">
  <h2>Support &amp; Organizations</h2>
  <p>Finding verified, moderated spaces matters. Avoid unmoderated social media groups that tend to amplify anxiety or spread unvetted advice.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="https://vasculitisfoundation.org/living-well/find-support/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Vasculitis Foundation Support Groups:</strong></a> Free virtual support groups, including weekly general sessions and monthly evening meetings.</li>
    <li><a href="https://connect.mayoclinic.org/discussion/gpa-granulomatosis-with-polyangiitis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Mayo Clinic Connect GPA Forum:</strong></a> Active, moderated community space for practical peer advice.</li>
    <li><a href="https://vasculitisfoundation.org/living-well/community-voices/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Community Voices:</strong></a> First-hand essays on managing fatigue, kidney care, and daily life with chronic illness.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
